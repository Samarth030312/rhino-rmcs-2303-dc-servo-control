## Speed PI designed for zeta 0.7 (Kp 1.0, Ki 2.9)
First step 0 -> 30 rpm: overshoot **33.3 %** on the rig, 25.8 % in the model; the textbook design predicted 4.6 %. After it the loop does not settle: it holds a **limit cycle of +/- 6.3 rpm at 30 rpm and +/- 6.6 rpm at 40 rpm** (model RMSE 4.63 rpm, the model shows the same cycle). The design ignored the 70 ms Modbus latency and the 100 ms sampling; with them, Kp = 1.0 on encoder speed leaves too little phase margin. A design for this rig must include the delay (or use a lower Kp: the hand-tuned Kp 0.3, Ki 1.5 settles).

![PI design](speed_pi_design.png)

## Saturation and windup (command limited to 50 rpm, 80 asked, then 30)
The output sits at the 50 rpm limit for 5.6 s while 80 is asked. When the setpoint drops to 30 rpm, the rig is back within 5 % in **0.5 s** (model with clamping 0.5 s). The same model with a plain integrator **does not get back within the remaining 6 s**: that difference is the windup the firmware's clamping prevents.
